## HDL-Optimized Filters and Transforms

- IFFT — Official summary: Compute inverse fast Fourier transform (IFFT) **metadata only; full English and Chinese documents pending**
- Channelizer — Official summary: Polyphase filter bank and fast Fourier transform **metadata only; full English and Chinese documents pending**
- Channel Synthesizer — Official summary: Combine narrowband signals into multichannel signal (Since R2022a) **metadata only; full English and Chinese documents pending**
- Discrete FIR Filter — Official summary: Finite-impulse response filter **metadata only; full English and Chinese documents pending**
- Biquad Filter — Official summary: Biquadratic IIR (SOS) filter (Since R2022a) **metadata only; full English and Chinese documents pending**
- LMS Filter — Official summary: Minimize error between observed and desired signals (Since R2023a) **metadata only; full English and Chinese documents pending**
- Farrow Rate Converter — Official summary: Polynomial sample-rate converter (Since R2022a) **metadata only; full English and Chinese documents pending**
- FIR Decimator — Official summary: Finite impulse response (FIR) decimation filter **metadata only; full English and Chinese documents pending**
- FIR Interpolator — Official summary: Finite impulse response (FIR) interpolation filter (Since R2022a) **metadata only; full English and Chinese documents pending**
- CIC Decimator — Official summary: Decimate signal using CIC filter **metadata only; full English and Chinese documents pending**
- CIC Interpolator — Official summary: Interpolate signal using CIC filter (Since R2022a) **metadata only; full English and Chinese documents pending**
- FIR Rate Converter — Official summary: Upsample, filter, and downsample input signal **metadata only; full English and Chinese documents pending**
- NCO — Official summary: Generate real or complex sinusoidal signals **metadata only; full English and Chinese documents pending**
- Complex to Magnitude-Angle — Official summary: Compute magnitude and phase angle of complex signal using CORDIC algorithm **metadata only; full English and Chinese documents pending**
- Downsampler — Official summary: Downsample by removing data samples between input samples (Since R2022b) **metadata only; full English and Chinese documents pending**
- Upsampler — Official summary: Upsample by adding zeros between input samples (Since R2022b) **metadata only; full English and Chinese documents pending**
