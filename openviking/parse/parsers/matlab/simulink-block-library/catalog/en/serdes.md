## Design and Simulate SerDes Systems / Signal Modification

- DFECDR — Official summary: Decision feedback equalizer (DFE) with clock and data recovery (CDR) **metadata only; full English and Chinese documents pending**
- CDR — Official summary: Models a clock data recovery circuit **metadata only; full English and Chinese documents pending**
- DFEClkFwd — Official summary: DFE with Clock Forwarding in the receiver AMI model (Since R2023b) **metadata only; full English and Chinese documents pending**
- FFE — Official summary: Models a feed-forward equalizer **metadata only; full English and Chinese documents pending**
- CTLE — Official summary: Models continuous time linear equalizer (CTLE) **metadata only; full English and Chinese documents pending**
- AGC — Official summary: Automatically adjusts gain to maintain output waveform amplitude **metadata only; full English and Chinese documents pending**
- VGA — Official summary: Models a variable gain amplifier **metadata only; full English and Chinese documents pending**
- SaturatingAmplifier — Official summary: Models a saturation amplifier **metadata only; full English and Chinese documents pending**
- IBIS-AMI clock_times — Official summary: Recover SerDes clock time values from custom DFECDR and CDR **metadata only; full English and Chinese documents pending**
- PassThrough — Official summary: Propagates baseband signal without modification **metadata only; full English and Chinese documents pending**
