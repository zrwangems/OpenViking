### **Ground and hide negative terminals** — Ground RF circuit terminals on (default) | off

Select this parameter to ground and hide the negative terminals. To expose the
negative terminals, clear this parameter. By exposing these terminals, you can connect
them to other parts of your model.

## Description

Compute sum of RF signals

The Signal Combiner block sums signals across each carrier frequency in
the circuit envelope simulation environment.
